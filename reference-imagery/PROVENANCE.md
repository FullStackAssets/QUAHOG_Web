## Files

- **ref-01-brick-warehouse-wet-cobblestone.jpg** — Tall red-brick warehouse, arched windows, wet cobblestone street with reflections. Source: confidentials.com (Manchester UK coverage — this is NOT a New Bedford building). Use: brick/warehouse material language + wet-cobblestone look ONLY. Do not treat as a local location reference.
- **ref-02-new-bedford-harbor-docks.jpg** — New Bedford Harbor: brick buildings, docks, fishing boats, calm reflective water. Source: media.gettyimages.com ("New Bedford Harbor"). LICENSED STOCK — visual reference only.

## Rules

- These images are for looking at while modeling, texturing, and lighting — never embed them as game textures or ship them in any build.
- Getty Images are rights-managed/licensed stock: reference-only, do not redistribute, do not trace into textures without a proper license.
- ref-01 is a UK building; use it for material/proportion language, not for geographic claims about New Bedford.
